# RoutlyX Support

For help, account/data requests or a community safety report, email [djaner.choban@icloud.com](mailto:djaner.choban@icloud.com). Include your app version, device model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

## Location and navigation

Allow location access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Routly. Enable Precise Location for navigation. Tap the recenter arrow if you have moved the map. Apple Maps routing needs an internet connection and coverage varies by region.

## Voice guidance

Check the mute control during navigation, the app's voice settings, your device volume and your Bluetooth output.

## Routly Pro

The optional monthly or yearly subscription enables Driving Assistant, acceleration timing, personal bests and Community chat. Basic navigation is free. Restore purchases from Settings or the paywall. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions, or Manage subscription in Routly Settings.

## Leaderboard, chat and road reports

Sign in with Apple in the driver profile and ensure the device is signed into iCloud. If a personal best has not synced, open Leaderboard and use Retry sync when available. Road markers require nearby matching observations from two different accounts. Reports expire and may not reflect current conditions.

To report or block someone in Community, open the message actions. You can also email the support address above.

## Account deletion

Open Leaderboard > driver profile > Delete account. This removes your cloud leaderboard records, messages and road reports, clears your profile and signs you out. Subscriptions are managed separately by Apple. Local personal bests can be reset in Settings.

## Using measurements

Acceleration and top-speed measurements are for closed courses and track days. GPS measurements can vary. Follow traffic laws, road signs and conditions; do not operate the app while driving.
