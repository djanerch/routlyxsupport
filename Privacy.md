# RoutlyX Privacy Policy

Updated: 30 September 2026

RoutlyX (called Routly inside the app) is operated by Dzhaner Choban. Contact: [djaner.choban@icloud.com](mailto:djaner.choban@icloud.com).

## Navigation and location

With permission, Routly uses precise location, heading and speed for maps, navigation, curve guidance and driving measurements. Active navigation can continue using location in the background. Apple Maps handles map, destination-search and routing requests under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Routly does not upload a continuous journey history to its CloudKit database.

When you choose to share a road report, its coordinates, report type, creation and expiry times, and an iCloud account identifier are uploaded to the app's public Apple CloudKit database. These records can be accessed by other users. Sharing is optional. An expired report stops appearing in the app; expiration does not itself guarantee immediate deletion of the stored record.

## Accounts and public features

Basic navigation does not require sign-in. Optional online features use Sign in with Apple and Apple iCloud/CloudKit. Apple may provide an account identifier and your name; Routly does not request your Apple password or email address through sign-in.

When signed in, your personal-best records can sync to the public leaderboard. Records include an iCloud identifier, display name, optional car and country, result, category and achievement date. Your name, car, country and result are visible to other users. Your profile photo remains on your device.

Community chat stores your messages, account identifier, display name, optional country and posting time in CloudKit. Messages are public to community users. Reporting a message sends its text, the reported author's identifier, the reporter's identifier, reason and date to the moderation service. Blocking preferences are stored on your device. Please avoid posting private information.

## On-device storage and widgets

Preferences, recent destinations, local driving records, your profile and photo, and subscription status are stored on your device. The leaderboard widget shares cached rankings and account-related state with the Routly app through an Apple App Group. It does not independently upload your location.

## Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. Routly uses StoreKit to check purchase and subscription entitlement information. Routly does not receive your payment-card details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

## Advertising and analytics

Routly does not include advertising or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs and does not use your data for cross-app tracking. Cloud data described above is used to provide the app's features and moderation, not to sell advertising.

## Your choices and deletion

You can change location permission in iOS Settings. You can edit your profile and remove its photo in the app. To delete your app account, open Leaderboard > driver profile > Delete account. This removes your cloud leaderboard records, chat messages and road reports, clears the local profile and signs you out. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase your local driving records. Local personal-best records can be reset in Settings. For assistance or requests concerning retained moderation/support records, contact the email above. Support correspondence includes the information you choose to send us and is used to respond to your request.

Cloud records remain until deleted through the app or removed as part of service administration. Apple may retain backups or service records under its policies. A public message or record may have been copied by another user before deletion.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
